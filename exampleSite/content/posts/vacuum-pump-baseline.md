The replacement seal went in Monday. Pulldown to 4.1e-6 torr took nineteen
minutes against a documented twenty-six, which is the first time this pump has
beaten its own datasheet since it arrived. The number is not the point. The
point is that the previous seal had been costing us seven minutes a cycle for
an unknown number of months, and nothing in the log would have told us, because
nobody writes down the time a thing takes when the thing is working.

Baseline recorded here so the next person has something to be suspicious of.
