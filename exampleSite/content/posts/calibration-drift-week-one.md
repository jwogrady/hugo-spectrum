Seven days against the reference cell: +0.4%, +0.4%, +0.5%, +0.5%, +0.6%,
+0.6%, +0.6%. Monotonic, decelerating, and small enough that a weekly check
would have reported it as flat twice and then as a step change once.

A drift that only shows up at the sampling rate you happened to choose is a
property of the sampling, not of the instrument.
