Twelve hours at 45C to see whether the enclosure holds its geometry. It does,
to within the resolution of what we can measure, which is a weaker statement
than it sounds and is the honest one.

What it cost: a full day of the chamber, and the discovery that the chamber's
own logger writes local time without an offset, so the overnight record folds
in half at the DST boundary. Not this week's problem. Written down anyway.
