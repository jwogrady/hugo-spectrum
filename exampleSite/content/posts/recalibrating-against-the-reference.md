Zeroed, warmed, and run against the cell three times with a twenty-minute gap.
The three agree to 0.1%, which is the tightest this instrument has ever been,
and the reason is embarrassing: the warm-up in the old procedure was eleven
minutes, and the instrument needs nineteen.

Eleven was in the procedure because eleven was in the manual for the model this
one replaced.
