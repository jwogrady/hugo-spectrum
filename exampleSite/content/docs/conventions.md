title  = "Recording conventions"
weight = 20
+++

**Times are the publication's, not the machine's.** Everything dated renders in
the configured zone. An entry filed at 01:00 UTC belongs to the previous day in
Chicago, and grouping on one zone while printing another files a record under a
day it is not shown to be on.

**Subjects are the index.** A tag is not decoration: its frequency sets its
colour, and colour is the only thing that makes a table of fifty entries
legible before a word is read. Tag what the entry is about and nothing else.

**Null results are recorded.** A log that only records incidents reads, a year
later, as though the instrument was broken continuously and briefly worked
twice.
