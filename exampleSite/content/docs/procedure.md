Nineteen minutes of warm-up, zeroed cold, then three runs against the reference
cell with a twenty-minute gap between them. The three must agree to 0.1%.

Every timing in this procedure carries the date it was measured and the entry
it was measured in. A step without a provenance is a rumour with a checkbox
next to it — see [JRN 26-010](/r/26-010/), which is what replaced the eleven
minutes this document used to specify.
