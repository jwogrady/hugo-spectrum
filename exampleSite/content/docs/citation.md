Each entry carries a handle — `JRN 26-015` — assigned once and never
renumbered. It resolves at `/r/26-015/` and it lives in front matter rather
than on the page: a handle is for citing an entry, not for reading on the way
into one.

Corrections are made in place, with the change and its date recorded at the
foot of the entry. Entries are never silently edited and never deleted, because
a handle that resolved once has to resolve forever.
