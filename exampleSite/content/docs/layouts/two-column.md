Main plus aside: 50.76rem and 17rem, with the gap, summing to the frame. The
aside is the page over phi cubed, which is where 17rem comes from.

## Use it for

**A page whose reader is still choosing.** The journal index, a tag result, a
page inside a branch. The main column answers what they asked; the aside says
what else is here.

**Anything with standing apparatus.** The subject field, the date archive, the
mission time, child navigation. These are not decoration — the spectrum is
how a table of fifty entries becomes legible before a word is read.

## What it costs

17rem of width, permanently. The main column is still 50.76rem, comfortably
over the 34rem measure, so prose does not suffer — but a wide table or a
full-bleed figure has less room than it would alone.

## How it is chosen

Automatic: the home page, any term page, anything with a `form`, and any page
inside a hierarchical branch. This page is in one, so it has the branch beside
it and chose two columns without being told.
