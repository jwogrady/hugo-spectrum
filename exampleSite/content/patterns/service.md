Calibration is not a certificate. It is three runs, twenty minutes apart,
against something whose own drift is known — and the value is entirely in
whether you can inspect the record afterwards.

This page carries `serviceType` instead of `sku`, which is the whole
difference between it and the product page. The layout does not change.
