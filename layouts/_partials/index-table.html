Dated records are grouped by day. Each day is a row group headed by its own date, and that head sticks to the top of the viewport while you scroll through the day's entries — so whatever you are reading, the date it belongs to is on screen. Newest first, so the latest is always at the top. Standing pages carry no date. A table of them gets neither the day heads nor the time column, and must never print Go's zero time: About was rendering 0001-01-01.
