{{- /* The revision record at the foot of a document. docs/citation.md has said since the theme existed that "corrections are made in place, with the change and its date recorded at the foot of the entry", and nothing implemented it. A convention a publication states and its own theme cannot render is a convention nobody can keep. A document and a record want different things here. A post is a record: what is published now is what matters, and a post edited after its date has simply been edited. A page is a document: which version a reader saw matters, and changing one silently is changing the standard without telling anyone. So this renders on documents and never on posts, and the caller decides which it is by whether it calls at all. version what to call the current state, printed as given revised when it last changed revisions a list of { date, note }, newest first — what actually changed All three are optional and independent. A page with `revised` and no `revisions` prints the date and no list, which is the honest rendering of "this changed and nobody wrote down what": visible, and visibly thin. Dates go through the publication's zone like every other date on the site, so a revision cannot file under a day the archive does not have. */ -}} {{- $tz := or site.Params.spectrum.timezone "UTC" -}} {{- $version := .Params.version -}} {{- $revised := .Params.revised -}} {{- $list := .Params.revisions -}} {{- if or $version $revised $list -}}
